How to play Flip

  • Each card has a Light and Dark face. Match color or number on the current side.
  • Flip turns every hand, draw pile, and discard to the other side.
  • Light: +1 / Wild +2. Dark: +5 / Wild Draw Color (draw until that color).
  • At 2 cards, tap UNO! before going to 1 — or risk Caught!
  • Flip rooms use a different code prefix than classic Uno — open the same Flip page.
