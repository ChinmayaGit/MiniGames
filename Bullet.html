How to play

Element weakness cycle

Clockwise beats the next. If they last used that element, your shot deals +50%.

Example: they last fired Fire → your Water hits harder. Fusions map to a base (Ice→Water, Lava→Fire, Plasma→Lightning, Stone→Earth, Tornado→Wind).

FUSION GUIDE

Fire · Water · Earth · Wind · Lightning. Twin & tri — any order. Tri counts fused Ice / Plasma / Lava / Stone / Tornado from your pairs.

Rules

  • Bullets (5): Tap to load into HAMMER (your 3 elements, random repeat).
  • FIRE: Single ⚡1 anytime. Twin ⚡2 / Triple ⚡3 only when HAMMER sequence matches a fusion recipe.
  • USE boosts: Power Up ⚡1 · Defence ⚡1 · Blind ⚡2 · Smoke ⚡1 · Speed Boost ⚡1.
  • Blind: enemy 50% miss · 10s. Smoke: enemy 100% miss · 5s. Speed Boost: your ⚡ fills twice as fast · 10s.
  • No turns — fire anytime. Low damage = longer duels. ⚡ refills every 5s.
  • Energy (⚡) fills 1 cell every 5s — need ⚡ to fire.
  • Element weakness: Last-used element shows on you and the enemy. Attacks that beat it deal +50% damage.
